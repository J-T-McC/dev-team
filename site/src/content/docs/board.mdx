Once several roles run at once, the orchestrator's conversation moves too fast to follow. The Owner board is your view instead: a private claude.ai page showing what's waiting on you, who's on what, and every piece of work in flight.

It works under either runtime. The team still works from `status.md`, and the board is built from it. Each work row is one task or ticket with one owner and its PR, so a feature being built shows as its tasks, grouped under the feature's name.

## Set it up

```
/dev-team:board
```

It needs Claude Code's claude.ai artifacts (the `Artifact` and `ArtifactData` tools). The command asks for a project name, what to call you, a ticket link base and the repository. Then it publishes the page, seeds it from `status.md`, and adds the `**Owner board:** <url>` line that turns it on. Running it again republishes to the same link and keeps the data.

The page is private unless you share it. People you share it with can read it, but only you can change its data (the orchestrator writes it as you). Nothing secret goes on it.

## What the states mean

| State | Means |
|---|---|
| `planning` | requirements through technical design |
| `building` | being built |
| `review` | in review |
| `fixing` | fixing review findings or a failed check |
| `ready` | reviewed, checks green |
| `owner` | waiting on you, with a matching question at the top |
| `blocked` | blocked on something the team must clear |
| `queued` | not started yet |
| `held` | you said to wait. Shown muted, and nobody asks again |
| `done` | finished. It stays until you've seen it, then goes |

A question or approval appears at the top the moment it's routed to you and disappears in the turn your answer arrives. Say "leave it until I've looked" and the row goes to `held`. Only you take it out.

## How it stays current

The orchestrator updates the board in the same turn as `status.md`. If it forgets, the plugin's Stop hook sends its turn back to do it.

PRs also move on their own. When one merges, fails a check or goes ready, `board-drift` finds its row for almost no tokens. Under Orca the watcher runs it every time it wakes.
